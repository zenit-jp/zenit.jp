Making every workplace a retreat

About ZenIT · 禅IT · Eiheiji town, Fukui

Can you imagine a world where every workplace is a peaceful retreat, where you leave every day feeling better than when you came in?

The ZenIT project explored a calmer, more productive way of working through pair programming, test-driven development, regular breaks and retrospectives. The project ran workshops and talks in 2018-2019.

The project grew out of concerns about workplace stress and long working hours. Through my experience working around the world, I have watched many of my good friends suffering in the workforce, where they were made to work 10-20 hours per day and on weekends, experiencing serious health problems. Even back home in the Silicon Valley, with shorter working hours, high stress, mental illnesses (anxiety, panic attacks, depression, etc.), and food issues (heartburn, etc.) are regarded as normal. Many people have worked in torment, despite being surrounded by beautiful things, technology, and ample finances.

We must also be aware that technology also poses a threat. Although it is convenient, human society is not fully aware of the side effects of heavy computer/smartphone use. These concerns prompted an interest in more deliberate, balanced technology use.

The workshops tested these ideas through practical programming exercises and participant feedback.

The founder’s background at the time included:

  • Experience with Silicon Valley software development practices, including pair programming
  • Eiheiji Town Evolution Ambassador
  • One Young World Ambassador
  • 14+ years of experience in Martial Arts, Yoga, and mindfulness

About Eiheiji and Zen

Fukui is continuously ranked as one of the happiest prefectures in Japan. Eiheiji is the head temple of Soto Zen, with 15,000 branch temples across the world. Up to 6,000 people per day visit it for sightseeing.

The workshops drew on Eiheiji’s local setting, with temple visits and zazen sessions alongside programming activities.
